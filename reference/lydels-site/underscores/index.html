about

underscores is a producer and songwriter from the united states. she has been releasing music since 2013 and she makes mainly pop & electronic music, though her unique sound is hard to fit into a single label. her music has it all: fun and interesting melodies, intricate and creative production, powerful and emotional vocals, and even more powerful and emotional lyrics. she has released three studio albums so far:

  • fishmonger [2021]
  • wallsocket [2023]
  • U [2026]

with the release of U, her latest album, she has become a truly iconic popstar. in 2026 she will be joining charli XCX for some shows of her tour, as well as solo touring in north & south america and europe, being her biggest world tour to date.
