roy mustang

when i started watching fullmetal alchemist brotherhood i didn't know i would end up finding one of my favorite animes of all time, and i also didnt know that after about 10 episodes this guy would start living in my mind rent free, but it seems he's not leaving anytime soon so i made this shrine for him

name roy mustang (ロイ・マスタング)
alias flame alchemist
birthday unknown (heartbreaking...)
age 28-30 (main series)
height 173cm

he's an easy character to love because he's just cool as hell but the way he constantly proves that there's so much more to him than just that as the story progresses was so entertaining to see. i really didn't care about him at first but as the episodes went by i totally fell in love LOL. it's nice to see a powerful character in anime that isnt reduced to the typical cold, stoic and/or detached personality. he cares a lot about people close to him and he makes sure they know that. he is obviously focused on his main goal but he also wouldn't doubt to put it all aside when someone important to him is in danger. i absolutely love characters who are very passionate about something or someone to the point where they would give it all up and risk everything just to protect that, and he's exactly that guy. he's so devoted and i love him grahhhhhhhhhh

i'm not gonna act like he isn't a pretty morally grey character who did terrible things lol. but what makes me really love him as a character is that even though his actions were in part guided by someone else, he never tries to make himself a victim or waste time defending his actions, and rather he decides to dedicate his whole life to making an impactful change in the world so the situation never repeats again. he doesn't waste time trying to undo, erase or forget the past. he chooses not to drown in guilt and regret, he moves forward, and i love that...

so because of his past, his arc mainly revolves around his main goal of becoming the leader of the country, a path he's chosen in order to atone for his mistakes. but what makes him a special character to me is that there are still so many more "sides" to him. for example he does have one big individualistic goal to achieve and a big burden to carry, but that doesn't exclude him from being a normal guy with simpler dreams and hopes living his life. i like that he doesn't isolate himself and that he wants better for his loved ones, and that he constantly relies on other people as other people rely on him. he values those around him and knows he wouldn't be able to accomplish anything on his own. i also like how whenever lives aren't in danger, politics are kinda just a game to him that he enjoys playing and it's so funny to see LOL. i love to see him do performances and play different roles in order to be a step ahead of everyone else. in those moments it feels like he doesn't take any of it seriously at all, it's more like he just wants to win the chess game and it's such a contrast from his more sentimental and obstinate side. i absolutely love ittt its so funny. he's such an interesting charcter. i also think he just really loves alchemy itself... his passion for it was probably killed by the war but i still think he genuinely enjoys snapping his fingers and setting shit on fire :P

royai

god i fucking LOVE THEMMMM MY PARENTSSSS i dont think i've ever seen such subtle but beautiful and powerful demostrations of love in any other anime, not even in romance ones... that one scene where they're talking over the phone and he immediatly notices something is wrong means so much to me LOL. the way roy is so perceptive when it comes to her safety and wellbeing KILLS meeeee, all the little ways he shows affection towards her are just the cutest ever... i love the way they can communicate without words, almost read each others minds. they are both bad at talking about feelings (maybe riza is a bit worse LOL) but it never feels like many words are needed between them. they have such a deep connection, it's really like there is no other person in the world who could ever understand them better than they understand each other.

i only watched brotherhood so this might be diferent in other media (i plan to read the manga and watch 03 at some point) but i would've really loved something to happen w them at the end. not even asking for a perfect happy ending, just some sort of closure? i know they can't just get married because of laws and blah blah but i would've still loved to see maybe one last scene of them talking about the future or something... also since this is roy and riza we're talking about, i am unable to believe that they couldn't keep a secret relationship if they wanted to lol. but i also understand that not having a happy ending is one of the consequences for their actions, as frustrating as that might be. either way the show does not fail in making it clear that they are soulmates and that nothing could ever separate them so i think i can live w that..
